# Awesome Bitcoin Culture [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of human creative work about Bitcoin — film, visual art, sculpture, literature,
> music, and the events that gather this scene together.

Bitcoin has produced a real body of creative and cultural work, separate from its use as money
or its developer tooling. This list catalogs that work: the people who made it, where to see it,
and the institutions that support it. It is deliberately **not** another `awesome-bitcoin`
dev-tools/wallets/services list — those already exist and are well maintained elsewhere.

## Scope

Bitcoin as subject and inspiration for human creative work — film, literature, visual art,
sculpture, music, and long-form media that engages with Bitcoin's ideas, history, or community.
This is not a directory of financial products, exchanges, or speculative collectible drops.

## Inclusion criteria

1. **Primary intent is creative, not financial.** A painting about Bitcoin qualifies. A PFP
   collection sold for profit doesn't, even if well-made, unless it clears #2.
2. **Attributable to a named human creator or identifiable studio/collective** — not an
   anonymous generative contract. (Memes & Internet Culture is the one deliberate exception —
   see that section.)
3. **Publicly viewable or verifiable.** The link must show or play the actual work.
4. **Someone can personally vouch for it.** Curation over comprehensiveness.
5. **Active or complete**, not an abandoned stub.

**Explicitly excluded:** NFT/ordinals PFP collections, exchange-marketing "art" campaigns, meme
coins, AI-slop generated in bulk.

### Institutions & Spaces

- [BMAG — Bitcoin Museum & Art Gallery](https://museum.b.tc) — Nashville, TN. Physical museum and
  auction platform, 170+ works, subsidiary of BTC Inc. Houses Hal Finney/Ross Ulbricht memorabilia.
- [Bitcoin Art Society](https://www.bitcoinartsociety.com) — Curator-led institution. Runs the
  Robert Mallary Art Residency; exhibits at NFC Summit, Bitcoin Asia, Ordinals Buenos Aires.
- [Bitcoin Art Gallery](https://bitcoinartgallery.com) — Physical gallery in Wynwood, Miami.
- [scarce.city](https://scarce.city) — Recurring commercial marketplace/auction hub; de facto
  backbone linking most named artists above.
- [Bitcoin Art Magazine](https://bitcoinartmagazine.com) — Separate publication from Bitcoin
  Magazine's own Culture vertical. *(Site has blocked automated access — needs manual check.)*
- [Bitcoin Magazine — Culture](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/culture) — Not dedicated to art but
  runs recurring art coverage, including annual conference gallery previews.

## Grants, Funders & Patronage

Organizations that fund or support Bitcoin-native creative work directly — grants, residencies,
sponsorships, patronage infrastructure.

- **[Bitcoin for the Arts (BFTA)](https://www.bitcoinforthearts.org/)** — Bitcoin For The Arts,
  Inc., a US 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN 41-2642260), the first paying working artists exclusively
  in Bitcoin. Runs quarterly BTC micro-grants, workshops, residencies, and co-productions across
  visual art, theater, dance, music, writing, storytelling, and film, under a public 55/30/10/5
  allocation rule (55% artist grants / 30% programs / 10% operations / 5% HODL Vault reserve).
  Founded 2025 by Dion Wilson (Founder & Executive Director, Interim Board Chair). Open source:
  [github.com/Bitcoin-For-The-Arts](https://github.com/Bitcoin-For-The-Arts/Bitcoin-For-The-Arts).
  Programs include Bitcoin Arts Park (a cultural footprint at Bitcoin conferences, first run at
  the Midwest Bitcoin Summit, Sept 2026, Columbus OH) and Art + Zap Weekend (a livestream +
  in-person fundraiser at Pubkey DC).

### BFTA partners & collaborators

- **[Midwest Bitcoin Summit](https://www.bitcoinforthearts.org/midwest)** — Hosts BFTA's Bitcoin
  Arts Park as a dedicated 30 ft cultural footprint on its Expo Hall floor (Columbus, OH,
  Sept 2026).
- **Dirty Coin** — Bitcoin mining documentary; featured screening presented inside Bitcoin Arts
  Park.
- **Ainsley Costello** — Performing artist; anchors the Secondary Stage performance lineup at

- **Kyle Knight** — Curator, leads the separately-run visual art gallery at the Midwest Bitcoin

- **The Bitcoin Way** — Self-custody education partner, in production on artist-facing education

- **Pubkey DC** (Washington, DC) — Hosts the in-person night of BFTA's Art + Zap Weekend
  fundraiser.
- **BFTA Board Advisors** — Buttercup Roberts, Kyle Knight, Julie Costello, Camas Logue, Valerie
  Love, Kenneth Burris.

## Platforms

Infrastructure that lets creators publish, distribute, or get funded directly in Bitcoin —
distinct from the grants/patronage orgs above, these are self-serve tools any artist can use.

- **[Geyser](https://geyser.fund)** — Non-custodial Bitcoin crowdfunding platform for creators,
  builders, and causes; supports rewards-based campaigns and recurring grants, paid entirely in
  Bitcoin/Lightning. Hosts campaigns for creative projects alongside broader Bitcoin-adoption
  causes (including Bitcoin for the Arts' own Midwest Bitcoin Summit sponsorship campaign).
- **[IndeeHub Studio](https://indeehub.studio)** — Lightning-enabled film streaming platform;
  filmmakers are paid in real time (per second watched) via a connected Lightning wallet, with
  ACH as a fiat fallback. No exclusivity required.
- **[Wavlake](https://wavlake.com)** / **Fountain** — Value4Value music and podcast streaming;
  artists earn Bitcoin Lightning "zaps"/"boosts" directly from listeners, often exceeding
  traditional streaming payouts. See Music above for artists active here.

## Events & Festivals

This list tracks creative work, not events — Tomek already maintains a dedicated, actively
updated calendar for that:

- **[Awesome Bitcoin Events](https://github.com/itstomekk/awesome-bitcoin-events)** — the
  canonical source for Bitcoin conferences, meetups, retreats, festivals, and technical
  gatherings worldwide, including Bitcoin FilmFest.

One exception worth naming directly here, since it's culture-first rather than industry-first:

- **[Revolution.Rocks](https://www.revolution.rocks/)** — Two-day music-and-culture festival on
  Belgrade's floating club Zappa Barka. Night one is a live Value4Value concert (Lightning
  micropayments direct to performing artists, no platform cut), night two is panels on
  technology and creator independence. Live-streamed free on Tunestr.
